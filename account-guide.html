Before You Get Started

GOLD QUEEN EA is an Expert Advisor designed for Gold trading on MetaTrader 5. It runs on a compatible MT5 desktop terminal or VPS environment. The Android and iOS MT5 mobile apps do not run Expert Advisors.

1

Prepare a Trading Account

You need a live trading account with a broker that supports MetaTrader 5 and the required XAUUSD trading conditions. Check the broker's contract specifications, spreads, leverage and execution conditions before using the EA.

2

Prepare MetaTrader 5

Install the desktop version of MetaTrader 5 on a compatible Windows computer or VPS. Keep the terminal running and connected to the internet while the EA is operating.

3

Request GOLD QUEEN EA

Contact the official GOLD QUEEN EA admin to request access and receive the relevant EA file and setup instructions.

4

Install and Configure

Follow the official installation instructions provided with your EA access. Use the supplied configuration and do not change protected strategy settings.

5

Monitor Your Trading Account

Check your account regularly. Monitor equity, margin, open positions and trading activity. Automated trading does not remove the need for supervision.

⚠️ Important Risk Information

GOLD QUEEN EA uses martingale and hedging techniques. These can increase exposure and may lead to substantial losses. You can lose some or all of your trading capital. Only use money you can afford to lose. No profit is guaranteed.
