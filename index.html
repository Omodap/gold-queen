BEFORE YOU TRADE

Understand the settings.

Do not run an automated strategy with default settings until you understand its entry rules, lot progression, maximum positions, hedge behaviour and exit conditions.

  • Test across different market conditions
  • Set a maximum acceptable drawdown
  • Use funds you can afford to lose
  • Monitor execution, spread and slippage
IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Risk disclosure.

Automated trading does not make trading risk-free. Please read this before using GOLD QUEEN.

Martingale and hedging strategies carry substantial risk.

Martingale systems may increase position sizes after losses, causing exposure and drawdown to grow rapidly during prolonged adverse market moves. Hedging can lock in losses, increase margin usage and incur additional spreads, commissions or swap charges. Fast markets, gaps, slippage, broker execution limits and insufficient margin may cause losses greater than expected, including loss of the trading account. Backtests and past results do not guarantee future performance. GOLD QUEEN is a software tool, not financial advice or a promise of returns. Always test on demo, read the EA documentation and use risk limits you understand.
